Our process

How we approach project work

For each project we follow four steps, scaled to the size of the job.

  1. Imagine

    We get to know your business, your users and what success looks like.

  2. Strategy

    We define the solution, the scope and a realistic delivery plan.

  3. Build

    We develop and test in small, reviewable increments, keeping you informed throughout.

  4. Launch

    We release, hand over clearly, and support the system after go-live.
