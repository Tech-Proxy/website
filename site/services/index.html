Services

Engineering help for every stage of your software

From working out what to build, to building it, to keeping it running well: four services, each delivered to an agreed scope.

01

Software analysis & solution design

Good software starts with a clear understanding of the problem. We work with your stakeholders to analyse requirements and define a solution that fits your business, your budget and your existing systems.

  • Requirements analysis and solution definition
  • Technical design and architecture for new features or systems
  • Reviews of existing code and systems, with practical recommendations
  • Estimates and delivery plans you can rely on
02

Web applications & APIs

We design and develop web applications and the APIs behind them, from new products to new features in established platforms.

  • Web application development, front end and back end
  • API design and development, and third-party integrations
  • Integrating AI services, such as Azure AI and large language models, into business applications
  • Clean, tested, documented code your team can maintain
  • Deployment to Microsoft Azure and AWS
03

Testing, debugging & upgrades

When existing software is unreliable, slow or out of date, we find the root cause and fix it properly.

  • Troubleshooting and debugging production issues
  • Regression, automated and manual testing
  • Framework and dependency upgrades
  • Refactoring to reduce risk and maintenance cost
04

Development support & SEO

Ongoing development support for the systems and websites your business depends on.

  • Development support for business and management information systems
  • Website maintenance and improvements
  • Technical SEO: performance, structure and metadata
  • Flexible monthly support agreements
