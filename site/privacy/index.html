Privacy notice

How Tech Proxy Ltd handles personal data, and your rights.

Who we are

Tech Proxy Ltd (company number 13354803, registered office 17 Green Lanes, London N16 9BS) is responsible for the personal data described here. For any privacy question, email onur@techproxy.co.uk.

What we collect and why

  • Emails you send us: your name, email address and anything you include. We use them to reply to you.
  • If we work together: business contact details of the people we deal with, and contract and invoice records. We use them to deliver the work and keep the accounting records the law requires.
  • When you visit this website: our hosting provider processes technical data, such as your IP address, to deliver and secure the site. We do not keep it.

We do not sell personal data, use it for automated decision-making or send marketing emails. You do not have to give us anything to browse this website, but we need your email address to reply to you.

We keep enquiries that do not lead to work for up to 2 years, and contract and financial records for 6 years, as HMRC requires.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. The map on our contact page comes from Google Maps and only loads if you choose to show it. Google may then set cookies under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or restrict your data, or for a copy of it. Email onur@techproxy.co.uk and we will reply within one month.

Your right to object: you can object at any time to our use of your data based on legitimate interests.

Complaints

Email onur@techproxy.co.uk with the subject “Data protection complaint” and we will acknowledge it within 30 days. You can also complain to the UK data protection regulator.
