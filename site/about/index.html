About us

A small consultancy with a hands-on approach

Tech Proxy Ltd was founded in April 2021 to provide software analysis, design and development services to businesses.

Who we are

Hands-on engineering from a director-led consultancy

Tech Proxy was founded in April 2021 by Onur Vatan, who owns the company and is its sole director. He is a software consultant and a Microsoft Certified Azure Developer Associate, and leads the company's projects from scoping to delivery.

Since 2021 Tech Proxy has delivered software analysis, development, testing and support services to businesses.

How we operate
  • Focused. We limit concurrent projects, so your work gets proper care and attention.
  • UK based, working remotely. We work remotely from the UK on our own equipment, with clients wherever they are, using whichever collaboration tools suit the project.
  • Transparent. Clear scope, regular updates, and no surprises on timelines or invoices.
  • Properly set up. A UK limited company, VAT registered, with Companies House and HMRC filings up to date.
